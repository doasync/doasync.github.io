### New Section 8.3. Resolved Regressions

- **Description:** Briefly mention the key regressions that were fixed, indicating how the `chat-stream`'s design or its correct integration contributed to the resolution.
  - **Message Regeneration:** `chat-stream`'s callback-based approach allowed consumers (like `chat/model.ts`) to implement logic for _updating existing messages_ (`targetMessageId`) rather than always adding new ones, resolving the duplication issue.
  - **Localized Loading States:** `chat-stream`'s `streamChatFx.pending` being a global indicator, and consumers managing their _own_ loading states via specific events (`streamRequestInitiated`, `chatStreamFinished`), allowed for accurate UI feedback without global interference.
